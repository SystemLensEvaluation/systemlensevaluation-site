Privacy Policy

SystemLens Evaluation — Tester Platform
Effective Date: April 26, 2026
Last Updated: April 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

SystemLens Evaluation operates the tester platform at systemlenstesters.netlify.app. We recruit and manage independent testers who evaluate mobile applications on behalf of our clients.

Contact for privacy matters:
privacy@systemlensevaluation.com

2. What Data We Collect

When you create a tester account, we collect:

Identity and contact information
Full name, email address, phone number

Demographic information
Date of birth, gender, race/ethnicity, education level, vocation, primary language, geographic location (country and region/ZIP)

Device information
Phone make, model, operating system and version, screen size and resolution

Technical and behavioral information
Tech comfort level, smartphone usage frequency, accessibility features you use daily, any self-described qualities relevant to diverse testing

Payment information
Wise account email or phone number for payment processing

Test-related data
Your responses to test assignments, screen recordings you choose to share via third-party links, screenshots you upload, and any written feedback

NDA records
A timestamped record of your agreement to any Non-Disclosure Agreement associated with a specific test assignment

3. Why We Collect This Data

We collect and process your data for the following purposes:

— To match you with test assignments appropriate to your profile and device
— To receive, store, and review your test submissions
— To process payments for completed assignments via Wise
— To send you assignment invitations and platform announcements based on your email preferences
— To maintain NDA compliance records on behalf of our clients
— To improve our tester matching and reporting processes

Legal basis (for EEA/UK users): We process your data on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you agree to our privacy policy at signup, and on the basis of legitimate interests in operating our testing business.

4. Sensitive Data

Some of the information we collect — including race/ethnicity, date of birth, and accessibility information — is considered sensitive personal data under GDPR and similar laws. We collect this data solely to enable demographic diversity in our tester panels. You are never required to provide this information, and you may leave any sensitive fields blank without affecting your ability to participate.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in Supabase, with servers located in Mumbai, India. Screen recording files are stored on third-party services you choose (such as Dropbox or Google Drive) and we only store a link you provide. Uploaded screenshots are stored in Supabase Storage, also in Mumbai.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or other regions with data transfer restrictions, please note that your data may be processed in countries that do not have an equivalent level of data protection. By using our platform, you consent to this transfer.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your profile and test data for 12 months after your last activity on the platform. After this period, your data will be deleted unless you have an outstanding payment, an active NDA obligation, or have requested an extension.

NDA signature records may be retained for longer periods if required by our contractual obligations to clients. You may request earlier deletion at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

— Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
— Correction — update inaccurate or incomplete data (you can do this directly in your profile)
— Deletion — request that we delete your account and associated data
— Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
— Withdrawal of consent — withdraw your consent at any time
— Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@systemlensevaluation.com. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA): We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to know what data we collect and to request deletion.

Japanese residents (APPI): We handle your personal information in accordance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information. You may request disclosure, correction, or cessation of use of your data by contacting us at the address above.

8. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

— Row-level security on all database tables, meaning each tester can only access their own data
— Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission
— Authentication managed by Supabase with industry-standard practices
— Sensitive fields such as payment information are stored only in our secured database and never shared with clients

9. What We Share With Clients

Our clients receive aggregated and anonymized reporting based on your test submissions. We do not share your name, contact information, or any individually identifying information with clients. Demographic information is presented in aggregate percentage form only.

NDA signature records confirming that a tester agreed to a specific NDA may be disclosed to the relevant client if legally required.

10. Children

Our platform is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us at privacy@systemlensevaluation.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered testers of significant changes by email. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
