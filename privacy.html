1. Who we are

System Lens Evaluation (システムレンズ評価) is a sole proprietorship registered in Japan, operated by Kirsi Erin Page. We provide independent software evaluation and user testing services through the platform at systemlensevaluation.com.

For the purposes of UK GDPR and data protection law applicable to UK and European data subjects, System Lens Evaluation is also affiliated with Hidden Insight Labs Ltd (UK Company No. 16941841), which holds ICO registration number ZC149156.

Two types of users This privacy notice covers two distinct groups: clients (companies and individuals who commission software evaluations) and testers (individuals who participate in evaluation panels). Some sections are relevant to both; where obligations differ, we note this clearly.

2. What data we collect

From clients

  • Name, company name, work email address, and job title
  • Information about your software product and evaluation requirements (provided via the contact form or during engagement setup)
  • Billing information — collected and processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card details (see Section 6)
  • Communications with us (email, platform messages)
  • Signed agreements and NDAs

From testers

  • Name, email address, and country of residence
  • Device information (device make, model, operating system, and OS version)
  • Skills, experience, and accessibility requirements (provided in your tester profile)
  • Test submissions, screenshots, and written feedback produced during evaluations
  • Payment details for evaluation compensation — collected via Wise; we store only the email address or phone number associated with your Wise account
  • NDA agreement records (timestamp, IP address, agreement version)
  • Communications with us

From website visitors

  • Aggregated analytics data via Google Analytics 4 (anonymised; see Section 11)
  • Information submitted via the contact form (name, email, company, and message)

3. How we use your data

Clients

  • To communicate with you about your enquiry and scoping your evaluation
  • To set up, manage, and deliver your engagement
  • To process payments and issue invoices via Stripe
  • To fulfil our contractual and legal obligations
  • To send you evaluation reports and deliverables

Testers

  • To operate your tester account and match you to appropriate assignments
  • To deliver evaluation tasks and collect your submissions
  • To process compensation payments via Wise
  • To maintain records of your NDA agreements
  • To notify you of new assignments (if you have opted in)
  • To assess writing ability and suitability for evaluation tasks (based on profile information you provide)

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary to deliver our services.

Service providers acting as processors

  • Payments — Stripe, Inc. (US). See Section 6 for full details.
  • Tester payments — Wise Payments Limited (UK/EU). We share tester Wise account identifiers to process evaluation compensation. Wise acts as an independent financial institution under its own regulatory obligations.
  • Platform hosting — Netlify, Inc. (US; transfers covered by Standard Contractual Clauses). The System Lens Evaluation platform is hosted on Netlify.
  • Database — Supabase, Inc. (US; transfers covered by Standard Contractual Clauses). Tester profiles, assignments, and submission data are stored in Supabase.
  • Transactional email — Resend, Inc. (US; transfers covered by Standard Contractual Clauses). Used to send assignment notifications and platform communications.
  • Email — Proton Mail AG (Switzerland; end-to-end encrypted). Used for client and internal communications.
  • File storage — Dropbox, Inc. and Google LLC (US; transfers covered by Standard Contractual Clauses / IDTA).
  • Website analytics — Google Analytics 4 / Google LLC (US; transfers covered by Standard Contractual Clauses / IDTA). See Section 11.

Clients and testers — limited sharing

Client details (company name and product description) are shared with testers only to the extent necessary to complete an assigned evaluation, and only after an NDA has been signed. Tester identities are not disclosed to clients; evaluation findings are attributed anonymously (e.g. "Tester T-03").

6. Payments & Stripe

Payment processing for client engagements is handled by Stripe, Inc., a PCI DSS-compliant payment processor. When you make a payment, you are providing your payment details directly to Stripe — we do not receive, store, or have access to your full card number, CVV, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Stripe may collect and process the following on our behalf:

  • Payment card details (handled entirely by Stripe's secure environment)
  • Billing name and address
  • Transaction amounts and timestamps
  • Device and browser information (for fraud prevention)

Stripe is an independent data controller for its own fraud prevention and compliance purposes. You can read Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

We retain records of transaction amounts, dates, and associated client identifiers for our accounting and tax obligations (see Section 8).

Stripe's compliance Stripe is certified to PCI DSS Level 1, the highest level of payment security certification. Transfers of payment data to the US are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. International transfers

System Lens Evaluation is operated from Japan. Japan has received an adequacy decision from the UK (under Article 45 UK GDPR), meaning the UK recognises Japan's data protection standards as equivalent. No additional safeguards are required for transfers between the UK and Japan.

Several of our service providers are based in the United States. We ensure transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or, where applicable, UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) approved by the ICO.

Our tester network includes individuals located globally, including in the EU, UK, Japan, and other countries. We apply UK GDPR protections to all data subjects regardless of location.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, if you are a UK or European data subject, you have the following rights:

Right of access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to rectification

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data about you.

Right to erasure

Ask us to delete your data where there is no longer a lawful reason to hold it.

Right to restrict processing

Ask us to pause processing your data in certain circumstances.

Right to data portability

Receive your data in a structured, commonly-used format.

Right to object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Right to withdraw consent

Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based (e.g. email notifications).

Rights re: automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

Japanese data subjects also have rights under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), including the right to disclosure, correction, and suspension of use of personal information. These rights can be exercised using the same contact details in Section 13.

We will respond to all rights requests within one calendar month. We will not charge a fee in most cases.

10. Security

  • End-to-end encrypted email (Proton Mail) for sensitive client and tester communications
  • Encrypted database storage via Supabase with row-level security
  • Multi-factor authentication on all accounts holding personal data
  • Payment data handled entirely by Stripe's PCI DSS Level 1 environment — we never store card details
  • NDA records include IP address and timestamp to support legal enforceability
  • Access to personal data restricted to authorised personnel only

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours where required and inform affected individuals without undue delay.

11. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymised data about how visitors use the site. Data collected includes pages visited, time on page, and general geographic region (country level). IP addresses are anonymised before storage.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — understanding site performance using minimal, anonymised data.

Opt out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or use a privacy extension that blocks analytics trackers.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting, or any tracking beyond GA4. The platform (systemlensevaluation.com) uses technically necessary cookies only — session tokens required to keep you logged in to your tester or admin account. These are strictly necessary and do not require consent.
