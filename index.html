How It Works

Four steps from brief to report.

No SDK. No instrumentation. No access to your codebase. We observe your product the way a real customer would — because we are one.

01
You Brief Us

Tell us your target user, what success looks like in your app, and any flows you're concerned about. We scope the evaluation from there.

~30 min call or async
02
We Assemble Evaluators

We recruit a panel matched to your real user profile — across technical ability, industry familiarity, and device. Not just power users.

Diverse panel
03
Evaluators Use Your Product

We give them real tasks and let them attempt to complete them without handholding. We capture where they hesitate, fail, and what they say out loud.

Structured sessions
04
You Get the Report

A clear, prioritised report of friction points, conversion blockers, and confusion patterns — written for your team to act on immediately.

Actionable output

Why System Lens

The perspective your team can't give itself.

When everyone on your team knows the product deeply, you stop seeing what's actually hard. We don't know your app. That's the point.

Not Your Beta Testers

Beta testers are self-selected, motivated, and already bought in. Our evaluators are none of those things. They behave like real customers — because they are.

Start Simple, Go Deeper

Our lightest engagement needs zero access to your systems — evaluators sign up and use your product exactly as a real customer would. For teams who want richer data, we also offer tool-assisted testing via platforms like TestFlight, Google Play Console, and similar — layering in structured metrics alongside human observation.

All Skill Levels, Represented

Your least technical customers are often your highest-value ones. We include evaluators who have never seen a tool like yours — and document exactly where they get lost.

Independent by Design

We have no stake in your roadmap. We report what we see — clearly, without softening the parts that are uncomfortable. That's what makes it worth paying for.

The simplest engagement requires nothing from your team. No SDK, no credentials, no onboarding us to your stack. Evaluators find your product the way a customer would and we report back. If you want more — larger panels, platform analytics via TestFlight or Google Play Console, or structured task scripting — we scale up from there. You decide how deep to go.

What You Get

Reports your team can actually use.

Every engagement ends with a structured written report. No raw session recordings to parse yourself. Prioritised findings, written for product and engineering teams to act on.

Friction Map

Every point where evaluators slowed down, hesitated, took a wrong path, or gave up — annotated by frequency and severity.

View sample report →
Conversion Blockers

The specific moments where evaluators decided — consciously or not — that this product wasn't worth continuing with. Prioritised by impact.

Direct User Language

Verbatim reactions, confusion statements, and questions your evaluators asked out loud. Useful for copy, onboarding, and support docs.

Performance Observations

Slowness and loading issues that evaluators noticed and reacted to — even when they couldn't name what was wrong technically.

Prioritised Recommendations

A ranked list of what to fix first, framed around business impact. High-frequency issues and high-severity blockers clearly separated.

Executive Summary

A one-page overview of the full picture — shareable with stakeholders, investors, or your board without needing to read the full report.
